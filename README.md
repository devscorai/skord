### Built for communities

Skord brings the tools your Discord server needs into one place. Moderate conversations, automate repetitive tasks, and create a better experience for your members.

**Features**

- **Moderation & AutoMod** — Keep your community safe and organized.
- **Tickets** — Manage support requests from your members.
- **Levels** — Reward activity and participation.
- **Temporary voice channels** — Give members their own voice spaces.
- **Welcome & roles** — Make onboarding easier.
- **Giveaways & more** — Keep your community engaged.

### Getting started

1. Visit [skord.dev](https://skord.dev).
2. Add Skord to your Discord server.
3. Configure your community through the dashboard.
